# Toza Visual Reference Shortlist

Status: **DIRECTION SELECTED — Evidence Ledger approved 2026-08-28; photography rights clearance remains open**  
Date: 2026-08-28  
Authority: `docs/visual-upgrade-decision-tree.md` remains the sole decision register. This file records research and recommendations, not locked decisions.

## 1. Non-negotiable brief

- The site must feel like a refined private advisory dossier, not a SaaS page, wealth manager, law firm, luxury brochure, or conventional cybersecurity vendor.
- Supporting visuals must help explain hidden exposure relationships.
- Use the existing founder portrait plus only two or three decisive ambient images.
- Ambient photography: ordinary busy people, approximately 7–21 in frame, walking, standing, or sitting in a street or mall; most people unidentifiable; subtly Israeli or a visually comparable Mediterranean city; no landmarks.
- Permitted sources: licensed premium stock and licensed existing editorial photography.
- No generated images. No hacker, lock, shield, code, surveillance, staged distress, or isolated worried-person-with-phone imagery.
- Code-native typography, rules, masks, and geometry are permitted, but every treatment must follow a documented precedent and a repeatable system.

## 2. Direction A — Evidence Ledger — RECOMMENDED

### Documented precedents

1. [Deloitte Insights by Pentagram](https://www.pentagram.com/work/deloitte-insights)
   - Documented method: a visible grid inspired by tax ledgers, bold typography, a restrained palette with one accent, and a governed family of visualization templates.
   - What Toza borrows: rigor, order, annotations, repeated evidence grammar, and a single accent that directs attention.
   - What Toza rejects: chart-heavy presentation, consultant handwriting, Deloitte green, and dense quantitative graphics.

2. [Texas Law Magazine by Pentagram](https://www.pentagram.com/work/texas-law-magazine)
   - Documented method: flexible grid, ample white space, chronological editorial architecture, and full-spread narrative “silos” that alternate proof, photography, and text for pacing.
   - What Toza borrows: dossier pacing, asymmetric editorial composition, decisive photographic interruptions, and clear section identities.
   - What Toza rejects: legal vocabulary, magazine mimicry, institutional branding, and decorative illustration.

3. [Rare Violins of New York by Pentagram](https://www.pentagram.com/work/rare-violins-of-new-york)
   - Documented method: authentic photography of the real expert and real work establishes artistry, provenance, and personally delivered expertise; elegant serif typography supports timeless authority.
   - What Toza borrows: the founder portrait as operational proof and the principle that authentic human presence must show who performs the work.
   - What Toza rejects: object worship, product luxury, and reverential workshop atmosphere.

### Toza application

- Light-first bone background, dark ink text, and one controlled vermilion accent.
- A visible but quiet editorial grid. Rules and junctions follow fixed spacing and alignment; they never become decorative circuitry.
- The three compound exposure families use one repeated evidence grammar:
  1. ordinary inputs;
  2. points of connection;
  3. plainly named consequence.
- One wide ambient crowd photograph interrupts the exposure section. The image represents ordinary life; the overlay or adjacent ledger reveals the hidden layer.
- The founder portrait receives a quieter, more formal spread with one sourced proof sentence and direct-delivery copy.
- Method and offer sections use ruled sequences rather than card grids.
- Situation pages inherit the same structure, but the exposure ledger and photographic crop change with the emotional context.

### Strengths

- Explains the new service category rather than merely decorating it.
- Makes compound exposure appear serious, structured, and professionally manageable.
- Supports the approved CXL conversion sequence without looking like a formulaic landing page.
- Creates a system that can extend cleanly into Hebrew and supporting pages.

### Primary risk

If overdrawn, it becomes academic or consultancy-like. Control this through large plain-language consequences, sparse annotations, generous space, and only three exposure paths.

## 3. Direction B — Human Intervention Editorial

### Documented precedents

1. [Rare Violins of New York by Pentagram](https://www.pentagram.com/work/rare-violins-of-new-york)
   - Authentic expert photography carries most of the authority and emotional weight.

2. [The Cleveland Orchestra by Pentagram](https://www.pentagram.com/work/the-cleveland-orchestra)
   - Documented method: typography modulates from quiet to loud while the identity deliberately avoids obvious category motifs.
   - What Toza borrows: strong typographic changes in intensity and explicit rejection of cybersecurity clichés.

3. [Texas Law Magazine by Pentagram](https://www.pentagram.com/work/texas-law-magazine)
   - Full-image narrative silos create pacing between long editorial passages.

### Toza application

- Founder portrait and three ambient images carry the page rhythm.
- Large editorial headlines alternate with short, tightly ruled explanatory sections.
- Compound exposure appears as three short textual chains with minimal geometry.
- The method reads as a forceful first-person sequence: Map, Close and harden, Verify.
- The palette remains bone, ink, and vermilion, with less visible grid structure than Direction A.

### Strengths

- Warmer, more personal, and emotionally immediate.
- Strongest expression of exclusive founder delivery.
- Lower risk of appearing technical.

### Primary risk

It does not explain compound exposure as convincingly. Without the evidence system, the site can drift toward a private adviser, law firm, therapist, or wealth-management aesthetic.

## 6. Current-site elements to change under either direction

- Replace the automatic dark/gold “vault” presentation. It signals luxury cybersecurity and changes with the visitor's operating-system setting, weakening deliberate art direction.
- Use light-first bone/ink/vermilion as the controlled primary experience. A later dark theme must be explicitly designed, not automatically inferred.
- Remove or sharply demote the guilloche, medal, seal, and gold-vault motifs. They do not explain hidden exposure and make the service feel ornamental.
- Replace boxed card grids with editorial bands, ruled sequences, and evidence-led layouts.
- Keep the founder portrait, but integrate it as proof of direct delivery rather than an About-page ornament.
- Use the ambient photographs only as decisive narrative interruptions; never as generic section backgrounds.
- Use motion only to reveal sequence or relationship. Respect reduced-motion preferences and never animate for atmosphere alone.

## 7. Decision result

The user selected **Category clarity first**. Therefore:

- **Evidence Ledger is locked as the governing direction.**
- Human photography remains sparse and purposeful: proof and pacing, not the organizing system.
- The Human Intervention Editorial direction is rejected as the governing system because it explains the category less clearly and risks a generic private-advisory aesthetic.

The exact grid, type roles, palette values, exposure-chain grammar, photographic placements/crops, responsive behavior, RTL behavior, motion, accessibility, and component inventory are defined in `docs/toza-production-implementation-spec.md`.
