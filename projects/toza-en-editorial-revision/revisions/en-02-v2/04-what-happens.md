# What happens during the visit.

You work directly with Dr. Lior Tabansky. You stay present, keep control of your credentials and approve material changes before they happen. We trace the relevant access, explain what it allows and carry out the work you have agreed to.

The first paid visit and the full protection service are separate stages. Here is what each includes.

In person · With you present · You control your credentials

## Before the visit

### 1. Start with a short, neutral message

Contact us through WhatsApp or Signal. Dr. Tabansky replies personally as soon as possible. The first conversation checks fit, urgency, location and practical arrangements. It includes no device inspection, mapping, diagnosis or technical work. Contacting Toza does not commit you to purchase.

Suggested message: **“I'd like a private conversation. Please let me know the next step.”**

Do not send passwords, recovery codes, screenshots, legal documents, intimate material or details, or detailed allegations in the first message.

### 2. Read the written scope and price

If the service fits, we send a written offer setting out the work, inclusions, exclusions, price, agreed additions and payment terms. The initial visit is scheduled after you explicitly accept and payment is received. Preparation instructions come directly from us.

## The first paid visit

**Private Exposure Assessment · ₪3,600 including VAT**

**At least two hours in person. Typically four.**

### 3. Explain your situation and map the relevant access

We start with what changed, what concerns you, which accounts matter and who previously had legitimate access. The work covers one main phone and one other device: a PC, iPad or second phone, together with relevant identities, accounts, apps, permissions, recovery routes and connected services.

We examine what those connections allow within your authorized environment, using what is disclosed, observable or otherwise lawfully accessible within the agreed scope.

### 4. Address urgent exposure with your approval

Where we find urgent exposure that can be addressed safely within the agreed scope, we make the approved changes during the visit. We explain what a material change does before acting.

Changing access can affect another device, a shared service or a recovery route. We consider the sequence with you. If an unrelated serious issue emerges, or further work would expand the scope or price, we explain it and obtain your approval before that additional work begins.

### 5. Receive a practical summary and decide what comes next

You receive a clear summary of what was reviewed, what urgent exposure was found, what we changed, what remains, whether broader protection is justified and the next responsible step. You receive it even if you buy nothing further.

The initial visit completes the agreed intake, access mapping, diagnosis and approved urgent first aid. Broader protection is a separate purchase decision. We explain the work needed and its scope and price; you choose whether to continue.

[See the first visit in detail](/en/private-exposure-assessment.html)

## If you choose broader protection

### 6. Agree the next engagement

Personal Shield carries out broader hardening for one person. Inner Circle Shield coordinates protection for four people and one included shared home or home-office network and router. The first Assessment covers the initial client; review and mapping for the remaining group belong to the separately purchased Inner Circle engagement. Bespoke Private Protection has an individually agreed scope and price when different work is needed.

We explain the recommendation and set out the work and price in writing before you decide. The full ₪3,600 Assessment fee is credited toward Personal Shield, Inner Circle Shield or Bespoke Private Protection if the selected engagement begins within 14 days of completion of the initial service. Booking alone does not qualify. The credit applies once to a group engagement; agreed additions are separate.

[Compare services, inclusions and prices](/en/pricing.html)

### 7. Carry out and verify the agreed work

Personal Shield and Inner Circle include device and account hardening, essential migration where needed, security-key setup, training and verification. We test material changes and verify the applicable work against the checklist. You learn how to sign in and recover access, and receive a private dated record. Historical archives and bulk migration require a separately agreed scope. A technically blocked or declined action is not presented as a completed change.

For Inner Circle, each participant receives a private record; the shared-network work is recorded separately. Personal details are shared only with that participant’s consent.

## After the protection work

Personal Shield includes 14 days of limited adjustment support for issues directly related to Toza’s changes, through WhatsApp, Signal and scheduled calls. Inner Circle’s 14-day period begins when the group’s protection work is complete and covers all four people and the included network, for issues directly related to Toza’s changes.

Annual Shield Review and Priority Retainer are optional, individually quoted follow-up services after a completed full service. They are separate from the included adjustment support.

## Your approval and information

You control your passwords, recovery codes and authentication; Toza does not keep them for ongoing use or remotely control your devices. We work within your authorized environment. We do not investigate another person, reconstruct past events forensically, collect legal evidence or give legal advice. An attorney-friendly summary is shared only with your authorization. The dated record is not certification or a guarantee that no exposure remains.

## Need an urgent visit?

We confirm availability before you pay. For an accepted four-hour urgent booking in Central Israel, arrival is within four hours of both Toza’s acceptance and receipt of payment. The clock starts when both conditions are met. This is an arrival commitment, not completion within four hours. Outside Central Israel, arrival time and travel terms are agreed before you accept.
