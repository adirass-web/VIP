## Other pricing and credit

### Larger engagements

Larger households, several residences or household staff: **from ₪90,000**. Scope and one fixed price are agreed in writing after the Assessment.

### Annual Care

For past clients after the 60-day question line: **₪12,000 a year** for one person · **₪15,000 a year** for two · **₪20,000 a year** for a household of up to four.

### Urgent visits

Availability is confirmed before payment. The urgent surcharge is **₪4,800**; weekends and holidays are **₪6,000**, replacing the ordinary surcharge. With the ₪4,000 Assessment, the totals are **₪8,800** or **₪10,000**.

### Assessment credit

The ₪4,000 Assessment counts toward protection if the work begins within 30 days of Assessment completion. Remaining balances: **₪28,000** for Personal Shield · **₪41,000** for Personal Shield for Two · **₪66,000** for Inner Circle. The credit applies once to a group engagement. After 30 days, the Shield is charged at its full price.
