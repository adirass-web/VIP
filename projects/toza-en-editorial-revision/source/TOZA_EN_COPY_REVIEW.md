You work directly with Dr. Lior Tabansky. You stay present, keep control of your credentials and approve material changes before they happen. We trace the relevant access, explain what it allows and carry out the work you have agreed to.

## Before the visit

### 1. Start with a short, neutral message

Contact us through WhatsApp or Signal. Dr. Tabansky replies personally as soon as possible. The first conversation checks fit, urgency, location and practical arrangements. It includes no device inspection, mapping, diagnosis or technical work. Contacting Toza does not commit you to purchase.

Suggested message: **“I'd like a private conversation. Please let me know the next step.”**

Do not send passwords, recovery codes, screenshots, legal documents, intimate material or details, or detailed allegations in the first message.

### 2. Read the written scope and price

If the service fits, we send a written offer setting out the work, inclusions, exclusions, price, agreed additions and payment terms. The initial visit is scheduled after you explicitly accept and payment is received. Preparation instructions come directly from us.

## The first paid visit

**Private Exposure Assessment · ₪3,600 including VAT**

**At least two hours in person. Typically four.**

### 3. Explain your situation and map the relevant access

We start with what changed, what concerns you, which accounts matter and who previously had legitimate access. The work covers one main phone and one other device: a PC, iPad or second phone, together with relevant identities, accounts, apps, permissions, recovery routes and connected services.

An account can still be open on an old device. A recovery address can provide another way in. Shared services can reveal messages, photos, documents or location information. We examine the relevant connections within your authorized environment, using what is disclosed, observable or otherwise lawfully accessible within the agreed scope.

### 4. Address urgent exposure with your approval

Where we find urgent exposure that can be addressed safely within the agreed scope, we make the approved changes during the visit. We explain what a material change does before acting.

Changing access can affect another device, a shared service or a recovery route. We consider the sequence with you. If an unrelated serious issue emerges, or further work would expand the scope or price, we explain it and obtain your approval before that additional work begins.

### 5. Receive a practical summary and decide what comes next

You receive a clear summary of what was reviewed, what urgent exposure was found, what we changed, what remains, whether broader protection is justified and the next responsible step. You receive it even if you buy nothing further.

The initial visit completes the agreed intake, access mapping, diagnosis and approved urgent first aid. Broader protection is a separate purchase decision. We explain the work needed and its scope and price; you choose whether to continue.

## The initial paid visit

### What does the first visit cover?

Private Exposure Assessment covers one main phone and one other device: a PC, iPad or second phone. We review the relevant digital identities, accounts, apps, permissions, signed-in devices, recovery routes and shared services, including access to private messages, photos, documents and location information.

We work only within your authorized digital environment, reviewing what is disclosed, observable or otherwise lawfully accessible within the agreed scope. This is not a promise to find accounts or devices that have not been identified.

### What do I get for ₪3,600?

The price includes VAT and direct, in-person work with Dr. Tabansky. We discuss your situation, trace the access that matters, explain what it allows and identify what needs attention first.

Where urgent exposure can be addressed safely within the agreed scope, we make the changes with your approval. You also receive a practical summary and ordered next steps. The work includes approved urgent fixes; broader protection work is a separate decision.

### How long should I allow?

At least two hours in person. A typical visit takes four hours. We complete the agreed intake, access mapping, diagnosis and approved urgent first aid. Four hours is a typical duration, not an automatic cutoff or a trigger for purchasing another service.

### What do I receive if I decide not to continue?

A clear summary of what we reviewed, what urgent exposure we found, what we changed, what remains, whether broader protection work is justified and what to do next. You receive it even if you buy nothing further. There is no automatic continuation to Personal Shield or another service.

We explain it and obtain your approval before addressing it. If the proposed work expands the scope or price, we agree that with you first. Finding an urgent problem does not authorize unagreed work or charges.

## Privacy and client control

### Do I give you my passwords or remote access?

You control your passwords, recovery codes and authentication throughout. Do not send them in advance. You may need to sign in on your own devices while we work. Toza does not keep your credentials for ongoing use or remotely control your devices.

### Will you see private messages, photos or documents?

Some information may be visible on your own screen where it is necessary for the agreed work, with you present. We explain the relevant access and agree what needs to be reviewed. The service is not a general inspection of your private content.

### Is cloud processing involved, and do you keep records?

Your accounts and shared services may themselves use cloud systems. During the core engagement, Toza does not upload a client dataset to a Toza cloud-analysis platform or retain a copy or comprehensive log of your digital life beyond agreed deliverables and ordinary lawful business records. This does not mean that no cloud services or administrative records exist.

### Can someone notice my contact with Toza or the changes we make?

Contact through WhatsApp or Signal is not guaranteed to be invisible on your device or account. Keep the first message short and neutral.

Some changes are visible. Ending a signed-in session, for example, signs that device out. We explain material changes and agree their timing and sequence with you before acting. We do not promise that changes will go unnoticed.

## Service boundaries

### Can you prove who accessed my account, investigate someone or collect evidence?

No. We examine your own authorized digital environment and reduce the exposure identified within the agreed scope. We do not investigate another person, attribute past activity, reconstruct events forensically, collect court evidence or give legal advice. Finding a current access path does not prove who used it or how information reached someone else.

### Is the dated record a certification or a security guarantee?

No. It is a practical, dated account of the work performed, not a forensic report, legal opinion, court-evidence document or certification. It does not guarantee that every possible exposure has been found or removed.

### Can my attorney or adviser be involved?

Yes, with your authorization. An attorney-friendly summary can be provided where useful, including after the initial service. An attorney's referral does not itself authorize access to your accounts or disclosure of your information. The summary does not turn the service into legal advice or evidence collection.

### How is this different from IT support or help from someone I know?

Toza focuses on how devices, identities, accounts and recovery routes connect in a consequential personal or business situation. Dr. Tabansky considers what access matters, what a change could affect and the order in which to act.

Someone you know may solve an individual device problem. This service provides a defined scope, direct senior work, client-controlled changes and a record or summary appropriate to the service purchased. It is not routine IT support.
