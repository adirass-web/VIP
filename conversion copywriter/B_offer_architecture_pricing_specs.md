# B0. Workstream Objective

The purpose of this workstream is to define exactly what Personal Cyber Shield sells, how each offer is positioned, how pricing is displayed, what is included, what is excluded, where scope is controlled, and how each offer supports immediate sales without turning the business into generic IT support.

This workstream must create a commercial architecture that:

1. Sells a clear entry product.
2. Supports premium pricing.
3. Prevents scope creep.
4. Keeps the buyer from thinking the service is self-fixable.
5. Makes the “from” prices credible.
6. Creates a clean path from assessment to full package.
7. Adds urgent/emergency pricing without making the business look like a 24/7 helpdesk.
8. Keeps recurring revenue attached to full-package clients.
9. Removes Home Shield for now.
10. Keeps Bespoke Private Protection visible only as a pricing-page line.

# B4. “Worth It / Not Worth It” Pricing Filter

## Purpose

This section should appear on the pricing page, the assessment page, and possibly the FAQ.

It has three jobs:

1. Filter out wrong-fit buyers.
2. Justify premium pricing.
3. Prevent the service from becoming generic tech support.

## Worth it

This is worth considering when:

- You are entering divorce, separation, inheritance conflict, business dispute, or another adversarial personal situation.
- Someone appears to know, assert, imply, or threaten something that should have stayed private.
- You believe your phone, accounts, apps, car, devices, cloud, messages, photos, or online trails may be exposed.
- You need a private expert to review the whole picture rather than panic-changing one password.
- Personal digital exposure could affect legal strategy, reputation, finances, safety, or family privacy.
- You need a dated private record of what was checked and changed.

## Not worth it

This is not for:

- Routine computer help.
- General tech support.
- Cheap device cleanup.
- Casual privacy curiosity.
- People looking for a quick remote fix.
- Password-manager setup only.
- Antivirus installation.
- Investigating another person.
- Forensic reconstruction.
- Offensive operations.
- Legal evidence collection.

## Required pricing-page copy block

> **When this is worth it**  
> This service is worth considering when private digital exposure could affect a divorce, separation, business dispute, inheritance conflict, safety situation, reputation, finances, legal strategy, or family privacy.  
>
> **When this is not worth it**  
> This is not routine computer help, general tech support, remote troubleshooting, or casual privacy advice. Personal Cyber Shield is for situations where guessing, partial fixes, or cheap support are not enough.

# B10. Urgent / Emergency Pricing

## Commercial role

Urgent pricing monetizes time sensitivity without turning Personal Cyber Shield into an always-on emergency helpdesk.

## Definitions

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Urgent handling** | Client requires response and possible in-person work faster than standard scheduling |
| **Emergency handling** | Within 4 hours, Central Israel |
| **Weekend / holiday handling** | Any engagement requiring work during weekend or recognized holiday |
| **Standard scheduling** | Normal appointment-based service |

## Emergency geography

Emergency pricing applies only to:

> **Within 4 hours, in Central Israel.**

Cases outside Central Israel are by arrangement.

## Pricing spec

The exact amount still needs confirmation.

Recommended surcharge architecture:

| Scenario | Public Display | Recommended Logic |
|---|---:|---|
| Urgent handling | Fixed surcharge | For accelerated scheduling |
| Emergency within 4 hours, Central Israel | Fixed surcharge | Highest urgency |
| Weekend / holiday | Extra fixed surcharge | Applies on top of applicable service |
| Weekend/holiday emergency | Emergency surcharge + weekend/holiday surcharge | Do not bundle for free |
| Outside Central Israel | By arrangement | Travel/time complexity |

## Recommended specific surcharge options

Choose one model:

### Option A — Conservative

| Scenario | Surcharge |
|---|---:|
| Urgent handling | +₪3,500 |
| Emergency within 4 hours, Central Israel | +₪7,000 |
| Weekend / holiday | +₪5,000 |

# B20. Final B Position

The offer architecture should be simple:

> **Start with a Private Exposure Assessment. If real intervention is needed, move into a full in-person Personal Shield package. If more people or unusual complexity are involved, scope it upward. Keep home network, vehicle, extra devices, urgent work, and recurring review as separate paid layers.**

This makes the business sellable now while protecting premium positioning, founder capacity, and margin.
