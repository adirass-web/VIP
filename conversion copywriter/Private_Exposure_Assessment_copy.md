## 5. Main sections

### 5.1 This is for you if…
- You are entering a divorce, separation, inheritance conflict, or business dispute.
- Someone appears to know, assert, imply, or threaten something private.
- You suspect your phone, accounts, apps, cloud, car, or devices may be exposed.
- You changed passwords but still do not trust the situation.
- Your attorney told you to be careful, but you do not know what that means digitally.

### 5.2 Why guessing is risky
Changing a password is not cyber protection. One visible problem rarely tells you the full picture, and a partial or mistimed fix can leave real exposure in place — or, in a sensitive situation, make things worse. The assessment is where you stop guessing and get an expert read on what actually matters.

### 5.3 What happens in the assessment
This is a paid diagnostic, not a sales call. With you present, we review your situation, map the accounts, devices, apps, services, and access points that matter to it, and identify what appears most urgent or sensitive. We explain what we find in plain English. Nothing is accessed remotely, nothing is uploaded, and no log of your digital life is kept.

### 5.4 What you receive
A brief plain-English summary of what was reviewed, what appears most urgent, and what should happen next — including whether a full package is justified and what you should not try to fix alone. Where you authorize it, an attorney-friendly summary can be provided.

### 5.5 Price and credit
**Private Exposure Assessment — ₪3,500.** Paid before scheduling. If you continue to a full package within 14 days, the assessment fee is credited in full.

### 5.6 Urgent handling
Urgent handling may be available by arrangement. Emergency handling within 4 hours in Central Israel carries a fixed surcharge of ₪4,200; urgent handling on a weekend or holiday carries a fixed surcharge of ₪5,800 (all-in). Cases outside Central Israel are by arrangement.

### 5.7 What this is not
Not routine IT support. Not investigation of another person. Not forensic reconstruction. Not legal evidence collection. Not legal advice. Not a remote fix. We reduce your own exposure; we do not look into anyone else.

### 5.8 How to make contact
Initial contact is by WhatsApp or Signal. Send only a short neutral message to start — no passwords, screenshots, legal documents, intimate details, recovery codes, or detailed allegations in that first message. A short template: *"I'd like to book a Private Exposure Assessment. The situation is [divorce / business dispute / inheritance / post-incident / other]. Please tell me the next step."*
