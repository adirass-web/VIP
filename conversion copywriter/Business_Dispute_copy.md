## 5. Main sections

### 5.1 The situation
A departing partner, a hostile shareholder, or the other side's advisors may know, copy, infer, or still reach personal accounts, documents, communications, devices, and online trails that overlap with the business. You may not know what is exposed, or whether old access was ever closed.

### 5.2 What can become leverage
The boundary between personal and commercial is rarely clean: a personal email tied to company logins, a shared drive, a phone that holds both, a recovery contact set years ago, an account a former partner still touches. In a dispute, any of that can quietly become leverage.

### 5.3 Why this is not enterprise cybersecurity
This is not a corporate security audit, a SOC, or a compliance project. It is a private, personal review of your own exposure in an adversarial moment — calibrated to your situation, done in person, with you present.

### 5.4 Start with an assessment
The Private Exposure Assessment maps what matters in your situation and tells you what's most urgent and what to do next. ₪3,500, credited in full toward a full package within 14 days.

### 5.5 Personal Shield or Bespoke
Most individuals need Personal Shield — a full in-person reset for one person, ₪14,000. Severe, unusual, or highly complex situations route to Bespoke Private Protection, by arrangement after the assessment.

### 5.6 Coordinating with your advisors
Where you authorize it, you can receive a plain-English, attorney-friendly summary of the work performed, and we can coordinate with your lawyer or advisor. It is not forensic reconstruction, evidence collection, or legal advice.

### 5.7 What this is not
We do not investigate the other side, reconstruct what happened, collect evidence, or guarantee that no exposure remains. We reduce your own exposure.
