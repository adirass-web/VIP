## 5. Main sections — the boundaries

### 5.1 We never take remote access
We do not take remote control of your devices. The work is done in person, with you present.

### 5.2 We never upload your private data to the cloud
Your private information is not sent to a third-party system for processing.

### 5.3 We never keep logs of your digital life
We do not retain a record of your accounts, messages, files, or activity. Full-package clients receive a private dated record of the work performed — which belongs to you and is not a log of your digital life.

### 5.4 We never investigate the other side
The service is about your exposure, not anyone else's behaviour. We do not look into, track, or gather information about another person.

### 5.5 We never run offensive operations
We do not access accounts, devices, or systems that you do not control.

### 5.6 We never reconstruct the past
We do not claim to prove what happened, how, or who did it. The work hardens your situation going forward.

### 5.7 We never give legal advice
We are not a substitute for your attorney. Where it helps, and only with your permission, we can coordinate with your counsel.

### 5.8 We never do routine IT support
This is not device repair or general tech troubleshooting. It is a focused reduction of personal digital exposure in a high-stakes situation.

### 5.9 We never guarantee that no exposure remains
We reduce the exposure we identify together. No honest service can promise that nothing remains — and we will not pretend otherwise.

### 5.10 We never rush a safety-sensitive case
If changing access could escalate danger, timing matters. The work follows your safety plan and your legal or safety advisor's guidance.

## 7. FAQ items (this page)
- **Can you find out if I was hacked, or who did it?** No. We do not investigate or reconstruct the past — we reduce your exposure going forward.
- **Can you investigate my spouse, partner, or business partner?** No. We only work on your own digital environment.
- **Can you collect evidence for court?** No. The deliverable is a private record of the work performed, not legal evidence.
- **Is this IT support?** No. IT support fixes devices; we reduce personal digital exposure when private information, access, or online trails could become leverage.
