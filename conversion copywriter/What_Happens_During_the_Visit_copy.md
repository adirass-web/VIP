## 5. Main sections

### 5.1 Before the visit
You reach us by WhatsApp or Signal with a short, neutral message — no sensitive details at this stage. We confirm whether your situation fits, agree a time, and tell you what to prepare. There are no forms and no third-party systems. If your case is urgent, emergency handling may be arranged. If your situation is safety-sensitive, timing and sequencing may be adjusted to keep you safe.

### 5.2 At the start
We begin with your situation — what has happened, what you are worried about, and what you fear may be exposed. Together we map the devices, accounts, apps, services, and access points that are relevant, and agree what will and will not be done in the session.

### 5.3 During the work
You remain present the whole time. We work through what matters, explaining each meaningful change in plain English before we make it. Nothing is accessed remotely, nothing is uploaded to a cloud system, and no log of your digital life is kept. Where we find exposure, we reduce or close it where appropriate. If something falls outside the agreed scope, it becomes a separate step or add-on — with your approval, never a surprise.

### 5.4 If something sensitive comes up
We stay calm and practical. We do not investigate anyone else, reconstruct the past, or give legal advice. Where it is appropriate, we may suggest you speak with your attorney, a safety advisor, or another specialist. If a change could create legal or safety risk, we pause and plan it with you rather than rushing.

### 5.5 At the end
You receive a clear, plain-English summary of what was reviewed, what changed, and what to do next. Full-package clients also receive a private dated record of the work performed, and — only if you authorize it — an attorney-friendly summary. After a full package, an Annual Shield Review may be offered.

### 5.6 What you leave with
Clarity instead of guesswork: you know what was checked, what was closed, what changed, and what still needs care. You leave more in control of your own digital life than when we arrived.

### 5.7 What this is not
This is not remote tech support, investigation of another person, forensic reconstruction, or legal advice — and it is not a guarantee that no exposure remains. It is a private, in-person reduction of your own digital exposure, done with you in the room.

## 7. FAQ items (this page)
- **Do I have to be there the whole time?** Yes — the work is done with you present. That is part of how it stays private and accountable.
- **Will you take remote control of my device?** No. There is no remote access and no cloud upload at any point.
- **Do you keep a copy of my data?** No. We keep no log of your digital life. Full-package clients receive a private dated record of the work performed — which belongs to you, and is not a record of your accounts or messages.
- **What if changing something could make my situation worse?** We pause and plan it with you. In safety-sensitive cases, timing matters more than speed.
