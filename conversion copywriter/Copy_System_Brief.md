## 3. Reusable copy blocks (from J3 / C13) — drop in as-is

**Trust strip:** In person. With you present. No remote access. No cloud processing. No retained logs.

**Private Exposure Assessment block:** Private Exposure Assessment — ₪3,500. A private in-person diagnostic for people who know something may be exposed but do not know where to look. You receive a brief plain-English summary of what was reviewed, what appears most urgent, and what should happen next. If you continue to a full package within 14 days, the assessment fee is credited in full.

**Worth-it / not-worth-it block:** *When this is worth it* — when private digital exposure could affect a divorce, separation, business dispute, inheritance conflict, safety situation, reputation, finances, legal strategy, or family privacy. *When this is not worth it* — not routine computer help, general tech support, remote troubleshooting, investigation, forensic reconstruction, or casual privacy advice.

**Certificate / record block:** Full-package clients receive a private dated record of the work performed and, where appropriate, an attorney-friendly summary. This is not a forensic report, legal opinion, evidence collection document, legal certification, or guarantee that no exposure remains.

**Emergency block:** Urgent handling may be available by arrangement. Emergency handling within 4 hours in Central Israel carries a fixed surcharge. Weekend and holiday work carries an additional fixed surcharge.

**Contact protocol block:** Initial contact is by WhatsApp or Signal. Please send only a short neutral message. Do not send passwords, screenshots, legal documents, intimate details, recovery codes, or detailed allegations in your first message.

**Not-IT-support block:** This is not computer help. IT support fixes devices. Personal Cyber Shield reviews personal digital exposure in high-stakes situations where private information, access, or online trails may become leverage.

**Not-DIY block:** Changing a password is not cyber protection. One visible problem rarely tells you the full picture.

**Safety-sensitive block (controlling-relationship pages):** If changing access could escalate danger, timing matters. The work must follow the client's safety plan and legal/safety advice where relevant.

## 5. Forbidden vocabulary & claims (from A11 + J2) — auto-fail in QA

Never use in public copy: hack-proof · absolute peace of mind · complete / total protection · complete digital boundary control · proof you are safe · proof of no compromise · court-ready / court-grade certificate · forensic report / forensic certainty · "for your legal file" (unqualified) · "we prove who accessed you" · "we find who did it" · "we investigate them" · "we monitor threats" · every hidden door closed · "one day, then it's closed" (except careful package time-box) · military / government-grade hardening · NSA / NIST / NCSC / DISA / STIG / IoC / attack surface / indicators of compromise · "forgotten access" / "old access" / "WhatsApp Web still open" / "Apple recovery contact" / "shared family plan" as hero copy · "world-leading" / "the only expert" (unless substantiated).

**Tone bans:** paranoia, spy-shop drama, fear-pumping, technical rabbit holes, long disclaimers above the fold, apologizing for price.
