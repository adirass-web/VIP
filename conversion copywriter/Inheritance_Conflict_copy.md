## 5. Main sections

### 5.1 The situation
When a family turns to conflict over an estate, caregiving, or money, access that was shared in good faith can suddenly matter: a sibling who knows your passwords, a shared family plan, a parent's device that holds your documents, a cloud folder everyone could once reach.

### 5.2 What can become leverage
Shared accounts, family device plans, joint cloud storage, recovery contacts, and the trails of years of family life can all become leverage when trust breaks — and you may not know which of them is still open, or who can still see what.

### 5.3 Why guessing is risky
Changing one password or leaving one family plan rarely tells you the full picture, and a partial or mistimed change can leave real exposure in place. This is interpretation under pressure, not a checklist.

### 5.4 Start with an assessment
The Private Exposure Assessment maps what matters in your situation and tells you what's most urgent and what to do next — ₪3,500, credited in full toward a full package within 14 days. If a full reset is needed, Personal Shield is the in-person reset for one person.

### 5.5 For your attorney, and what this is not
Where you authorize it, you can receive a plain-English, attorney-friendly summary of the work performed — not a forensic report, legal evidence, or legal advice. We do not investigate other family members, reconstruct the past, collect evidence, or guarantee that no exposure remains.
