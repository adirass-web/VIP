## 5. Main sections

### 5.1 The situation
In a contested matter, the other side may rely on ordinary digital exposure — public traces, location and travel trails, accounts and settings that quietly reveal more than you'd expect. You may not know what is visible, or how. The goal here is clarity about your own environment, not a confrontation.

### 5.2 What ordinary exposure can reveal
Much of what an opposing party can use isn't a hack — it's what's already visible: online trails, location history, recovery settings, shared services, and the digital residue of everyday life. Knowing what yours reveals is the first step to reducing it.

### 5.3 Why guessing is risky
Reacting in a hurry — changing settings, deleting accounts, going dark — can backfire or leave the real exposure in place. A private assessment tells you what actually matters, and what's safe to change and when.

### 5.4 Start with an assessment
The Private Exposure Assessment is a private, in-person review of what your own digital environment reveals — ₪3,500, credited toward a full package within 14 days. If a full reset is warranted, Personal Shield is the in-person reset for one person.

### 5.5 What this is not
We do not investigate the investigator or the other side, reconstruct the past, or guarantee that no exposure remains. We reduce your own exposure, calmly and discreetly.
