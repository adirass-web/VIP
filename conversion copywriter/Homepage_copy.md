## 5. Main sections

### 5.1 The real risk (core wedge)
They may not need to hack you. They may only need what your digital life already exposes.

Serious exposure rarely requires sophistication. It often comes from accounts, apps, devices, settings, online trails, recovery paths, and everyday access points that ordinary people cannot fully see or evaluate. When a relationship turns adversarial, that exposure can become leverage — and changing a few passwords rarely tells you the full picture.

> **You don't leave with more worry — you leave with clarity.** You stop guessing, and you know what was found, what changed, and what to do next.

### 5.3 Start here: Private Exposure Assessment
Private Exposure Assessment — ₪3,500. A private in-person diagnostic for people who know something may be exposed but do not know where to look. ### 5.4 How the visit works
You stay present. We review your situation, map the accounts, devices, apps, and access points that matter, explain what we find in plain English, and reduce exposure where appropriate. Nothing is done behind your back, nothing is uploaded, and no logs of your digital life are kept. → `How the visit works`

### 5.5 Why Personal Cyber Shield
Led by Dr. LT, the service brings the judgment of a senior cybersecurity expert to a private consumer problem: when a personal dispute turns adversarial, the most damaging exposure may not look like a hack. You are paying for expert interpretation of unclear exposure under pressure — not a checklist. → `Why us`

### 5.6 When this is worth it — and when it isn't
**Worth it** when private digital exposure could affect a divorce, separation, business dispute, inheritance conflict, safety situation, reputation, finances, legal strategy, or family privacy.
**Not worth it** for routine computer help, general tech support, remote troubleshooting, investigation, forensic reconstruction, or casual privacy advice.

### 5.7 What we never do
No remote access. No cloud processing. No retained logs of your digital life. No investigation of the other side. No forensic reconstruction. No legal advice. No guarantee that no exposure remains. → `Boundaries (FAQ #boundaries)`

## 7. FAQ items (homepage teaser)
- **Will you see my private messages or photos?** Only if it is necessary to reduce a specific exposure, always with you present and on your own screen — never opened for review, copied, uploaded, or retained.
- **Do I need to give you my passwords?** No. You stay in control of your own credentials throughout.
- **Can the other side tell that changes were made?** In sensitive situations, timing matters — we plan changes with your safety in mind rather than rushing them.
