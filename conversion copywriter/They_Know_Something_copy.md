## 5. Main sections

### 5.1 This page is for you if…
- They quoted, showed, or implied private information.
- They appear to know where you were.
- They know about messages, files, photos, accounts, or plans.
- You received suspicious login or access alerts.
- Your phone or accounts feel untrustworthy.
- You already changed passwords but still feel exposed.
- You do not know whether this was hacking, access, settings, apps, or online trails.

### 5.2 Don't panic-fix one thing
When something surfaces, the instinct is to change one password, delete one app, or pull one device offline. In a high-stakes situation that can leave the real exposure in place — and, if the timing is wrong, tip off the other side. One visible problem rarely tells you the full picture.

### 5.3 Why "maybe it was a hack" isn't where to start
Hacking is only one possibility, and usually not the likeliest. What someone knows may come from ordinary access, a setting, a shared service, a recovery path, or an online trail — not a break-in. Until the whole picture is reviewed, guessing which one it was just costs you time you may not have.

### 5.4 What a Private Exposure Assessment does
A senior cybersecurity expert reviews your situation with you, in person, and maps the accounts, devices, apps, and access points that matter — identifying what looks most urgent and what to do next. You stop guessing and get a clear, plain-English read on where you actually stand.

### 5.5 What it does not do
It does not prove how they got it, find who did it, recover what was shared, or promise that no exposure remains. It is not an emergency forensic service or an investigation. It reduces your own exposure going forward.

### 5.6 If it's urgent
Urgent handling may be available by arrangement. Emergency handling within 4 hours in Central Israel carries a fixed surcharge of ₪4,200. If that urgent handling falls on a weekend or holiday, the surcharge is ₪5,800 all-in (it replaces the ₪4,200, not added to it). Cases outside Central Israel are by arrangement.

### 5.7 How to make contact
Initial contact is by WhatsApp or Signal. Send only a short neutral message to start — no passwords, screenshots, legal documents, intimate details, recovery codes, or detailed allegations in that first message.
