# J1. Copy Strategy Principles

## Primary copy job

The copy must make the right buyer think:

> “This is my situation. I should not guess. I should book a private assessment.”

## The copy must not make the buyer think:

> “I can just check WhatsApp Web, change passwords, and fix this myself.”

## Core copy rules

| Rule | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Lead with situation | Divorce, dispute, inheritance, post-incident, controlling relationship |
| Lead with buyer fear | They know, see, infer, track, assert, or may expose something private |
| Sell expert diagnosis | Do not teach DIY |
| Keep technical language low | Technical details belong in process/SoW, not hero |
| Use plain consumer language | No standards/frameworks/acronyms in hero copy |
| Preserve premium tone | Calm, discreet, serious |
| Avoid paranoia | Do not sound sensational or spy-like |
| Avoid overclaim | No guarantee, no legal proof, no forensic certainty |
| Keep CTAs direct | Book Private Exposure Assessment |
| Filter wrong-fit leads | Not IT support, not investigation, not forensics |

# J3. Reusable Copy Blocks

## J3.1 Trust strip

Use on all core pages.

> **In person. With you present. No remote access. No cloud processing. No retained logs.**

## J3.2 Private Exposure Assessment block

> **Private Exposure Assessment — ₪3,500**  
> A private in-person diagnostic for people who know something may be exposed but do not know where to look. You receive a brief plain-English summary of what was reviewed, what appears most urgent, and what should happen next. If you continue to a full package within 14 days, the assessment fee is credited in full.

## J3.3 Worth-it / not-worth-it block

> **When this is worth it**  
> This service is worth considering when private digital exposure could affect a divorce, separation, business dispute, inheritance conflict, safety situation, reputation, finances, legal strategy, or family privacy.  
>
> **When this is not worth it**  
> This is not routine computer help, general tech support, remote troubleshooting, investigation, forensic reconstruction, or casual privacy advice.

## J3.4 Certificate / record block

> Full-package clients receive a private dated record of the work performed and, where appropriate, an attorney-friendly summary. This is not a forensic report, legal opinion, evidence collection document, legal certification, or guarantee that no exposure remains.

## J3.5 Emergency block

> Urgent handling may be available by arrangement. Emergency handling within 4 hours in Central Israel carries a fixed surcharge. Weekend and holiday work carries an additional fixed surcharge.

## J3.6 Contact protocol block

> Initial contact is by WhatsApp or Signal. Please send only a short neutral message. Do not send passwords, screenshots, legal documents, intimate details, recovery codes, or detailed allegations in your first message.

## J3.7 Not IT support block

> This is not computer help. IT support fixes devices. Personal Cyber Shield reviews personal digital exposure in high-stakes situations where private information, access, or online trails may become leverage.

## J3.8 Not DIY block

> Changing a password is not a digital safety reset. One visible problem rarely tells you the full picture.
