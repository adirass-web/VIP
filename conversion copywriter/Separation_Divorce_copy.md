## 5. Main sections

### 5.1 The situation
You may not know whether the other side can see, track, infer, or access private parts of your digital life — or whether they already have. You may suspect something but not know where to look, or fear that one wrong change will make things worse. That uncertainty, in the middle of a separation, is its own kind of pressure.

### 5.2 What can become leverage
In a separation, ordinary access can quietly become leverage: shared accounts and devices, family plans, cloud folders, location sharing, recovery settings, and the online trails of an ordinary life. You may not know whether what the other side seems to know came from hacking, ordinary access, a setting, an app, or a trail — and that is exactly the point.

### 5.3 Why guessing is risky
Changing a few passwords is not cyber protection. One visible problem rarely tells you the full picture, and a partial or mistimed fix can leave real exposure in place — or, in a contested separation, escalate things. This is interpretation under pressure, not a checklist.

### 5.4 Start with an assessment
The Private Exposure Assessment is the first step: a private, in-person diagnostic that maps what matters in your situation and tells you what's most urgent and what to do next. ₪3,500, credited in full toward a full package within 14 days.

### 5.5 If a full reset is needed
Personal Shield is the full in-person reset — one person, one day, one primary phone, every account identified together. ("Every account" means every account we identify together as relevant to your personal digital exposure — not a promise to find accounts you don't surface.) More people or complexity are scoped after the assessment.

### 5.6 For your attorney
Where you authorize it, you can receive a plain-English, attorney-friendly summary of the work performed. It is not a forensic report, legal evidence, or legal advice — and we coordinate with your counsel only with your permission.

### 5.7 What this is not
We do not investigate your spouse, reconstruct the past, collect material for legal proceedings, or guarantee that no exposure remains. We reduce your own exposure, discreetly, with you present.

### 5.8 How to make contact
First contact is by WhatsApp or Signal — a short, neutral message only. Please don't send passwords, screenshots, legal documents, or detailed allegations in that first message; sensitive details are discussed in the right setting, not in chat.
