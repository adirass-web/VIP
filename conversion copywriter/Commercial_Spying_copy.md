## 5. Main sections

### 5.1 The situation
A competitive conflict rarely stays neatly "commercial." The other side may reach, infer, or still have access to personal accounts, a phone that holds both work and private life, messages, files, and the online trails around a deal — and you may not know what is open.

### 5.2 What can become leverage
A personal email tied to business logins, a shared folder, a recovery contact, a device that holds everything, location and travel trails — in a commercial fight that has turned personal, any of it can be used against you.

### 5.3 Why this is not enterprise cybersecurity
This is not a corporate audit, a security-operations centre, or counter-espionage. It is a private, personal review of your own exposure, calibrated to your situation and done in person, with you present.

### 5.4 Start with an assessment
The Private Exposure Assessment maps what matters and tells you what's most urgent and what to do next — ₪3,500, credited toward a full package within 14 days. Most individuals need Personal Shield; complex cases route to Bespoke Private Protection, by arrangement.

### 5.5 What this is not
We do not investigate the other side, reconstruct what happened, collect evidence, or guarantee that no exposure remains. We reduce your own exposure.
