## 5. Main sections (the FAQ)

### 5.1 Privacy & how the visit works
- **Will you see my private messages or photos?** Only if it is necessary to reduce a specific exposure, always with you present and on your own screen — never opened for review, copied, uploaded, or retained.
- **Will you take remote access to my device?** No. The work is done in person; there is no remote control at any point.
- **Will anything be uploaded to the cloud?** No. Your private data is not sent to any third-party system for processing.
- **Do you keep logs or copies of my data?** No. We keep no log of your digital life. Full-package clients receive a private dated record of the *work performed* — which is yours, and is not a record of your accounts or messages.
- **Can the other side tell that changes were made?** Sometimes a change is visible by design (for example, ending a session signs that device out). In sensitive situations we plan timing and sequencing with you rather than acting abruptly.
- **Can my spouse, partner, or business partner tell that I contacted you?** Contact is by WhatsApp or Signal and kept minimal. We never reach out to anyone else, and there are no forms or third-party systems involved.

### 5.2 Passwords & first contact
- **Do I need to give you my passwords?** No. You stay in control of your own credentials throughout.
- **What should I send in the first message?** Just a short, neutral note — for example: *"I'd like to book a Private Exposure Assessment. The situation is [divorce / business dispute / inheritance / post-incident / other]. Please tell me the next step."*
- **What should I not send?** No passwords, screenshots, legal documents, intimate details, recovery codes, or detailed allegations in that first message. Sensitive details are discussed in the right setting, not in chat.

### 5.3 Pricing & payment
- **Why does the assessment cost ₪3,500?** It is a paid, in-person diagnostic with a senior cybersecurity expert — not a sales call. If you continue to a full package within 14 days, it is credited in full.
- **Is the assessment credited toward a full package?** Yes — in full, for any full package booked within 14 days.
- **Why is Personal Shield ₪14,000?** It is a full day of in-person expert work for one person and one primary phone, with every account identified together. More people, devices, or complexity are priced on top and scoped before any work begins.
- **What counts as urgent, and what does it cost?** Faster-than-standard scheduling, or emergency handling within 4 hours in Central Israel — a fixed surcharge of ₪4,200. If that urgent handling falls on a weekend or holiday, the surcharge is ₪5,800 all-in (it replaces the ₪4,200, it is not added to it).

### 5.4 The assessment & full packages
- **Is the assessment a sales call?** No. It is a paid diagnostic. You leave with a clear read on your situation whether or not you book anything further.
- **What do I actually get from it?** A brief, plain-English summary of what was reviewed, what appears most urgent, and what should happen next — including whether a full package is justified and what you should not try to fix alone.
- **What does Personal Shield include?** One person, one day, one primary phone, and every account identified together during the assessment or engagement — a full in-person reduction of your personal digital exposure, with you present. "Every account" means every account we identify together as relevant to your personal digital exposure — not a promise to find accounts you don't surface.
- **What if I need more than one device or person?** Additional devices, additional people, home network, and vehicle reviews are available as add-ons, or — for several people — as Inner Circle Shield. Scope is set before any work begins.

### 5.5 What we never do (boundaries) {#boundaries}
*(Canonical boundaries section — the retired "What We Never Do" page links here as `faq.html#boundaries`.)*
- **Can you find out if I was hacked, or who did it?** No. We do not investigate or reconstruct the past. We reduce your exposure going forward.
- **Can you prove who accessed my account?** No. That is forensic work, which this service does not do — we focus on reducing your exposure from here on.
- **Can you investigate my spouse, partner, or business partner?** No. We only ever work on your own digital environment, never anyone else's.
- **Can you collect evidence for court?** No. The deliverable is a private record of the work performed — not legal evidence and not a forensic report.
- **Is this IT support?** No. IT support fixes devices. We reduce personal digital exposure when private information, access, or online trails could become leverage.
- **Is this legal advice?** No. We are not a substitute for your attorney, and we do not give legal opinions.
- **Do you guarantee I'll be safe, or that nothing remains exposed?** No honest service can promise that. We reduce the exposure we identify together — and we won't pretend more than that.

### 5.6 Working with your attorney
- **Can you work with my attorney?** Yes, where it helps and only with your permission.
- **Will I get something my attorney can use?** Full-package clients can receive an attorney-friendly summary — a plain-English description of the work performed, provided only if you authorize it. It is not a forensic report, legal opinion, or evidence document.

### 5.7 The private dated record
- **What is the private dated record? Is it legal evidence?** It is a dated record of what was checked and changed during a full package — something tangible you keep. It is not legal certification, a forensic report, court evidence, or a guarantee that no exposure remains.

### 5.8 Why not just ask someone you know
- **Why not ask a tech-savvy friend or nephew?** This isn't about who can find a setting. It's about judgment, privacy, and discretion in a high-stakes situation. An informal helper can miss the wider picture, make a partial or mistimed change, or learn private things you'd rather they didn't — and turn a serious matter into family knowledge.

### 5.9 Safety-sensitive situations
- **What if changing things could make my situation worse?** Then timing matters more than speed. In controlling-relationship situations we work to your safety plan and your legal or safety advisor's guidance, and we don't rush access changes that could escalate danger.

### 5.10 After a full package
- **What is Annual Shield Review, and can I get it now?** It's a yearly review of what changed — new devices, accounts, access, travel, or household arrangements — and a chance to close what should no longer be open. It's offered only after a full package, not after an assessment alone.
