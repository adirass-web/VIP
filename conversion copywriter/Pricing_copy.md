## 5. Main sections

### 5.1 Start here — Private Exposure Assessment — ₪3,500
A private in-person diagnostic for people who know something may be exposed but do not know where to look. You receive a brief plain-English summary of what was reviewed, what appears most urgent, and what should happen next. Continue to a full package within 14 days and the assessment fee is credited in full.

### 5.2 Personal Shield — ₪14,000
One person. One day. One primary phone. Every account identified together during the assessment or engagement. A full in-person reset of your personal digital exposure, with you present throughout.

> **What "every account" means:** every account identified together during the assessment or engagement as relevant to your personal digital exposure — not a promise to find every account that could exist anywhere.

### 5.3 Inner Circle Shield — from ₪38,000
For situations where more than one person is exposed — a spouse and children, a founder and a close household member, several family members in a dispute. The exact scope is set after the Private Exposure Assessment.

### 5.4 Bespoke Private Protection — by arrangement
For severe, unusual, or highly complex situations that need a custom private scope. Discussed privately after an assessment.

### 5.5 Add-ons
Scoped before work begins; expansions during a visit need your approval first.

| Add-on | From |
|---|---:|
| Additional device (laptop, tablet, second phone) | ₪9,000 |
| Additional person | ₪12,000 |
| Home network / router | ₪5,000 |
| Vehicle access review | ₪5,000 |

### 5.6 Urgent & out-of-hours
Urgent handling may be available by arrangement.
- Emergency handling within 4 hours in Central Israel — **fixed surcharge of ₪4,200**.
- Urgent handling on a weekend or holiday — **fixed surcharge of ₪5,800** (all-in).
- Cases outside Central Israel are by arrangement.

### 5.7 After a full package
These are offered only to clients who have completed a full package.
- **Annual Shield Review — from ₪7,000/year.** Once a year, we review what changed — new devices, accounts, access, travel, or household arrangements — and close what should no longer be open.
- **Priority Retainer — from ₪14,000/year.** Priority scheduling and bounded advisory access for clients who want continuity.

### 5.8 When this is worth it — and when it isn't
**Worth it** when private digital exposure could affect a divorce, separation, business dispute, inheritance conflict, safety situation, reputation, finances, legal strategy, or family privacy.
**Not worth it** for routine computer help, general tech support, remote troubleshooting, investigation, forensic reconstruction, or casual privacy advice.

### 5.9 What is not included
This is a private cyber protection service. It does not include investigation of another person, forensic reconstruction, legal advice, routine IT support, or any guarantee that no exposure remains.

### 5.10 What you leave with
Full-package clients receive a private dated record of the work performed and, where appropriate, an attorney-friendly summary. It is a record of what was checked and changed — not a forensic report, legal opinion, evidence document, certification, or guarantee that no exposure remains.

### 5.11 Contact
Initial contact is by WhatsApp or Signal. Send only a short neutral message to start — no passwords, screenshots, legal documents, intimate details, recovery codes, or detailed allegations in that first message.

## 7. FAQ items (pricing page)
- **Why does the assessment cost ₪3,500?** It is a paid diagnostic with a senior expert, in person — not a sales call. If you go on to a full package within 14 days, it is credited in full.
- **Is the assessment really credited?** Yes — in full, toward any full package booked within 14 days.
- **Why is Personal Shield ₪14,000?** It is a full day of in-person expert work for one person, one primary phone, with every account identified together. More people, devices, or complexity are priced on top and scoped before any work begins.
- **What counts as urgent?** Faster-than-standard scheduling, or emergency handling within 4 hours in Central Israel. Each carries a fixed surcharge.
- **Do you work weekends or holidays?** By arrangement, with a fixed surcharge.
