## 5. Main sections

### 5.1 Safety first
If you are in immediate danger, contact local emergency services or a domestic-abuse support service before anything else. This page is about planning your digital safety calmly — it is not a substitute for a safety plan, an advocate, or your lawyer. `[CONFIRM: local IL support-org names/numbers to list here]`

### 5.2 Why timing matters
Changing a password, turning off location sharing, or removing a device can be visible to someone who is watching — and a sudden change can escalate the situation. Whether and when to make a change depends on your circumstances. The aim is to avoid tipping anyone off before you are ready and safe.

### 5.3 Clean ground first
Sometimes the safest first step is to set up new, separate ground — a device or account the other person has never had access to — rather than changing what they can already see. A safety-aware conversation can help you think this through before you act.

### 5.4 What a safety-aware conversation can do
With you, and in coordination with your safety plan or advocate where relevant, we can help you understand where you may be exposed, what is safe to change and when, and how to sequence it. Nothing is done remotely, nothing is uploaded, and no log of your digital life is kept.

### 5.5 What not to do abruptly
Please don't rush to "close everything" at once, confront the other person with what you've found, or assume a single change makes you safe. We won't promise that any change is invisible, or that it can all be done in one day — your safety comes before speed.

### 5.6 External support
Specialist domestic-abuse organizations, advocates, and your lawyer can help with safety planning, legal protection, and housing. We work alongside them, not in place of them. `[CONFIRM: which support resources to name]`

### 5.7 If and when you're ready
If it is safe to do so, you can reach out privately by WhatsApp or Signal with a short, neutral message — no details, no screenshots. We'll talk about timing and next steps at your pace.

## 7. FAQ items (this page)
- **Will they know I made changes?** Possibly — that's exactly why timing matters. We plan changes around your safety rather than rushing them, and sometimes the safest first step is new ground they've never had access to.
- **Can you do it all in one visit?** Not necessarily, and we won't pretend otherwise. In a safety-sensitive situation, sequencing safely matters more than speed.
- **Do I have to decide anything now?** No. A first conversation is just that — understanding your options at your pace.
