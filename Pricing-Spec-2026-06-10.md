# Pricing Spec — 2026-06-10 (Model B: bounded tiers + à-la-carte add-ons)

Source of truth for /pricing. Single fixed prices only (no ranges).

## Value metric
The **device** (and the **person**). **Primary device = the client's phone (iOS/Android); a laptop, tablet, or PC is an additional device.** State plainly on the page.
Labour (Lior): phone ≈8h · extra device 3–7h · home network 3–4h · implied ≈₪1,900/h. Add-ons absorb variance into a flat price.

## Tiers (BOUNDED) — SOW model, CANONICAL (VAT-incl). Reconciled into live EN pages 2026-06-10.
- Personal ₪14,000 — 1 person · phone · all accounts · 2 keys · certificate · coaching (~1 day)
- Home ₪33,000 — Personal + home control plane (router / network segmentation / smart-home accounts) + up to TWO additional devices. **Vehicle is an add-on, not in-tier.**
- Inner Circle ₪38,000 — up to 3 people, each phone + accounts (no environment)
- Sovereign ₪95,000 — most comprehensive / bespoke · counter-surveillance · Annual Watch + Retainer included

## Add-ons (any tier) — SOW prices
- Additional device ₪9,000 · Home network ₪5,000 · Vehicle ₪5,000 · Additional person ₪12,000
- Counter-surveillance sweep — by arrangement · Annual Watch ₪7,000/yr · Priority Retainer ₪14,000/yr

## Decisions
- Monitoring + Retainer (now "Annual Watch" + "Priority Retainer") = opt-in annual add-ons on ANY tier (bundled into Sovereign as a perk).
- Home bounded (fixes scope creep): home control plane + up to 2 devices; vehicle moved OUT to an add-on. Inner Circle = people only. Sovereign people = by arrangement; ₪95,000 kept as anchor.
- Tier-2 renamed **Extended → Home** to match the SOW. The public Scope page (`/en/whats-included`) derives from SOW Appendix A/B.
